Typical engagements

What a data warehouse project looks like in our industries.

How the work usually runs, starting with one team or one process.

Insurance: policy and claims history

Insurers often keep years of policy and claims history in systems that cannot be queried together. We build a warehouse for one line of business first, then add the rest.

What changes

  • History queried in one place
  • Faster month-end reporting
  • A base for pricing models

Oil and gas: production history

Production and maintenance history often sits in historians and spreadsheets. We model one asset's history in a cloud warehouse on Microsoft Fabric.

What changes

  • Years of history kept and searchable
  • One model for every report
  • Storage that grows with the data

Engineering and construction: project history

Completed project data is often lost when a project closes. We build a warehouse that keeps cost, schedule, and quality history for every project.

What changes

  • Lessons kept after handover
  • Estimates based on real history
  • Portfolio reporting on demand
