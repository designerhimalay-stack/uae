How we work

What process do we follow?

Four stages, from kickoff to ongoing managed support.

  1. 01

    Onboard and kickoff

    We set up the cloud environment, connect your systems, define scope, and agree security policies.

  2. 02

    Review and optimise

    Day-to-day management: monitoring, updates, and tuning for performance, availability, and reliability.

  3. 03

    Enhance

    Auto-scaling, resource allocation, and performance tuning that improve agility and find cost savings.

  4. 04

    Managed support

    Ongoing monitoring and troubleshooting, with expert support that keeps the platform secure and available.
