Each refinery client keeps inspection, NDT, and maintenance records in its own systems and formats.

Compliance reports rebuilt by hand

Teams assemble OSHAD, environmental, and client compliance reports from spreadsheets for every audit.

Turnarounds coordinated by email

Shutdown schedules, contractor crews, and inspection scopes sit in separate tools.

Without connected data, recurring defects and safety risks across facilities stay hidden.
