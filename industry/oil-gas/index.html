Typical engagements

What an oil and gas engagement looks like.

How first projects usually run for pipeline, well services, and inspection teams.

Pipeline maintenance: predictive integrity

Integrity teams often work to fixed schedules while SCADA data, sensor readings, and inspection logs sit in separate systems. We bring the feeds into one Microsoft Azure data platform, then add models that flag corrosion and leak risk before the next inspection.

What changes

  • Crews sent where the risk is
  • High-risk segments flagged sooner
  • Maintenance planned on evidence

Drilling and well services: one well data platform

Seismic surveys, well-test results, and field notes often live in separate places and are reconciled by hand. We consolidate them on Azure with Power BI dashboards that field and engineering teams use every day.

What changes

  • Faster access to well performance data
  • Quicker field decisions
  • Less manual consolidation

Refining and distribution: audit-ready inspection data

Turnaround, NDT, and inspection firms often keep each client site's records in a different format. We connect inspection, NDT, and compliance data into one reporting layer, starting with a single site.

What changes

  • One audit-ready reporting layer
  • Less reconciliation across facilities
  • Faster turnaround coordination
