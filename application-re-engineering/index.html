How we work

How our application re-engineering services work.

Re-architecting critical business applications is complex. Our process gives it structure, predictability, and expertise.

01

Audit and analysis

Our engineers examine your current application environment in depth: code, architecture, data flows, and integrations.

02

Design and planning

Detailed specifications for the future architecture, components, data structures, APIs, and user experience.

03

Engineering and migration

Agile teams build, integrate, and validate the re-engineered application against the blueprint, then migrate and test the data.

04

Post-implementation support

Continued support after deployment to tune performance and meet new needs as they arise.

Use cases

Use cases.

Where re-engineering pays off in the industries we work in.

Oil & Gas

Operations teams often run decades-old plant and field systems that disagree with the ERP. Re-engineering them onto one cloud data platform keeps the data in step and lays the foundation for AI forecasting.

Engineering & Construction

Project teams often depend on desktop tools that office, site, and client cannot share. Re-engineering them into secure cloud applications gives everyone the same current information.

Insurance

Insurers often serve customers and brokers through web and mobile channels built on outdated frameworks. Re-platforming them adds responsive design and self-service while the core systems stay in place.

Typical engagements

How a re-engineering project usually runs.

One application first, proved in production, then the rest of the portfolio.

Insurance: policy and claims core

We often see insurers whose policy and claims systems depend on mainframes and costly integrations. We re-architect one line of business on Microsoft Azure as a proof of concept, then roll out to the others while the existing system keeps running.

What changes

  • Fewer costly integrations
  • Faster changes to products
  • A core ready for data and AI

Engineering and construction: field and project apps

Contractors often rely on web apps that are unstable, hard to extend, and not built for phones. We rebuild the one site crews use most, prove it on a live project, then move the remaining apps onto the same architecture.

What changes

  • Stable apps for site crews
  • Code that is easier to extend
  • Screens that work on mobile

Why re-engineer

Why should you re-engineer your business applications?

Most legacy applications were built years ago and can't keep pace: inflexible infrastructure, poor scalability, limited integration, and rising costs. Re-engineering lifts these constraints so you can:

Scale quickly to meet surges in traffic and demand

Integrate with modern tools for better insight

Reduce costs by optimising resources

Deliver more features, faster

Improve stability, security, and compliance

Bring your apps into the modern age, with the agility and performance to keep innovating.
