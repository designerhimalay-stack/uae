Typical engagements

What a dashboard project looks like in our industries.

How the work usually runs, starting with one team or one process.

Insurance: broker performance

Underwriting teams often judge broker performance from exports and pivot tables. We build one dashboard on broker and policy data, then add claims.

What changes

  • Broker performance in one view
  • Figures everyone agrees on
  • Reports that refresh themselves

Oil and gas: safety reporting

HSE teams often compile incident and inspection figures by hand each month. We build a safety dashboard for one site from the systems already in place.

What changes

  • Incidents and inspections in one view
  • Trends spotted sooner
  • Less time compiling reports

Engineering and construction: tender pipeline

Bid teams often track tenders and win rates in spreadsheets. We build a dashboard of the tender pipeline, linked to project outcomes.

What changes

  • The whole pipeline in one view
  • Win rates by sector and client type
  • Lessons fed back into bids
