Typical engagements

What managed Azure looks like in our industries.

How the work usually runs, starting with one team or one process.

Insurance: policy and claims platforms

Insurers often rely on a small IT team to watch policy and claims platforms out of hours. We take over monitoring, patching, and backup for one environment first, with agreed response times.

What changes

  • Platforms watched around the clock
  • Patching on a schedule
  • Monthly reports on cost and security

Oil and gas: operations servers

Operations servers often go unpatched because nobody wants to risk downtime. We manage patching windows, backups, and alerts for one site's systems, with tested restores.

What changes

  • Patches applied safely
  • Restores proven before they are needed
  • Fewer surprises for operations

Engineering and construction: project systems

Project systems often grow with every new job and are rarely reviewed. We manage the Azure environment across projects, with cost reviews as projects open and close.

What changes

  • Costs that follow the project pipeline
  • One team for every project system
  • Issues fixed before sites notice
