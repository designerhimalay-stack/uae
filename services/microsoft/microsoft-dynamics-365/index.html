Typical engagements

What a Dynamics 365 project looks like in our industries.

How the work usually runs, starting with one team or one process.

Insurance: finance and commissions

Insurers often run finance, broker commissions, and customer records in separate systems. We implement Dynamics 365 in phases, starting with one process such as commissions.

What changes

  • One view of each customer
  • Commissions calculated in one system
  • Month-end with less manual work

Oil and gas: jobs to invoices

Service companies often track jobs, equipment, and invoicing in disconnected tools. We implement Dynamics 365 Field Service and Finance, proving the job-to-invoice flow on one service line first.

What changes

  • Jobs invoiced sooner
  • Crews and equipment scheduled together
  • Costs visible by job

Engineering and construction: project costs

Contractors often manage project costs in spreadsheets beside the accounting system. We implement Dynamics 365 Project Operations on one project, then connect it to finance.

What changes

  • Project costs tracked as they happen
  • Budgets and actuals side by side
  • Fewer spreadsheets to reconcile
