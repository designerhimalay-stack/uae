Typical engagements

What a Copilot project looks like in our industries.

How the work usually runs, starting with one team or one process.

Insurance: submissions and correspondence

Underwriters and claims handlers often spend hours summarising submissions and letters. We pilot Microsoft Copilot with one team, after checking who can see what, then extend it once the value is clear.

What changes

  • Faster summaries of long files
  • Drafts grounded in your own documents
  • Data access checked before rollout

Oil and gas: manuals and procedures

Engineers often search manuals, permits, and incident reports by hand. We build a Copilot Studio agent over one document library, so crews ask questions and get answers with sources.

What changes

  • Answers that link to the source
  • Less time searching manuals
  • Knowledge kept as people move on

Engineering and construction: project reporting

Project managers often rebuild the same reports and meeting notes every week. We set up Copilot in Teams and Word for one project team, with prompts shaped to their reports.

What changes

  • Meeting actions captured for you
  • Report drafts ready sooner
  • More time for site coordination

How we work

Our process.

Five stages, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1Step 01

    Onboard and kickoff

    We connect the cloud environment to your existing systems, define scope, choose services, set security policies, and link your applications.

  2. 2Step 02

    Review and optimise

    Day-to-day management: monitoring for issues, updating components, and keeping performance, availability, and reliability high.

  3. 3Step 03

    Enhance

    Auto-scaling, resource allocation, and performance tuning that improve agility and find cost savings.

  4. 4Step 04

    Managed support

    Monitoring, management, and troubleshooting around the clock, for a secure platform with high uptime.

  5. 5Step 05

    Customer feedback

    We act on your feedback: tracking issues, adjusting security settings, and installing updates promptly.
