AptaCloud UAE

Built around UAE rules, hosted in the UAE.

We set up AI, data, and cloud around the UAE's data protection and reporting rules, on Azure regions in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Azure regions in the UAE

  • UAE NorthDubai
  • UAE CentralAbu Dhabi
PDPL

Personal data protection

Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. We map where personal data lives and moves before anything goes live.

DIFC and ADGM

Free zone data rules

The Dubai and Abu Dhabi financial free zones have their own data protection laws. We design for the one you operate under.

FTA

VAT and tax invoices

Dynamics 365 finance set up for UAE VAT returns and tax invoices to Federal Tax Authority requirements.

WPS

Payroll reporting

Salary data prepared in the Wage Protection System format straight from your HR and finance systems.

Typical engagements

What a typical engagement looks like.

Three patterns we see with UAE businesses, and how we approach them.

A retail group

Stock ran out in peak seasons because inventory updates were unmanaged. We migrated the point-of-sale data, integrated SAP through secure APIs, and added role-based access in line with regional governance.

An insurance provider

Manual claims took days, with gaps in fraud detection. We added NLP document classification, moved data to the cloud, and built an AI tool for claims triage and underwriting.

A heavy equipment manufacturer

Frequent machine breakdowns delayed production. We built predictive maintenance models on a hybrid cloud with Azure IoT Hub and a Power BI dashboard.
